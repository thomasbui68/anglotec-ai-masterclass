AI Prompts for Business: Complete Guide to Automating Work

Published 2026-06-04 · Anglotec AI Masterclass

AI can automate 80% of routine business tasks — if you know the right prompts. This guide covers the essential prompts every business professional needs.

Market Research

  • "Analyze the competitive landscape for [industry] in [region]. Identify top 5 competitors, their strengths, and market gaps."
  • "What are the emerging trends in [industry] for 2026? Include data sources."
  • "Create a customer persona for [product] targeting [demographic]."

Content & Marketing

  • "Write a month-long content calendar for [industry] covering [topics]."
  • "Create 5 ad variations for Facebook targeting [audience] with [budget]."
  • "Write a press release announcing [news] for [company]."

Operations

  • "Create an onboarding checklist for new [role] employees."
  • "Draft a standard operating procedure for [process]."
  • "Analyze this quarterly report and highlight 3 key insights: [paste data]"
