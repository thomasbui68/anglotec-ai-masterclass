Our masterclass teaches you one prompt at a time through interactive flashcards. You listen to the prompt, practice speaking it back, and track your progress. It's like Duolingo, but for AI prompting.

5 Free Beginner Prompts to Try Right Now

1. Write an Email

"Write a professional email to [name] about [topic]. Keep it polite, under 150 words, and include a clear call to action."

Use this for: work emails, follow-ups, meeting requests, thank-you notes.

2. Summarize Anything

"Summarize the following text in 3 bullet points: [paste text here]"

Use this for: articles, reports, meeting notes, long emails, research papers.

3. Brainstorm Ideas

"Give me 10 creative ideas for [topic]. Each idea should be unique and practical."

Use this for: blog topics, marketing campaigns, product names, event planning.

4. Explain Like I'm 10

"Explain [complex topic] like I'm 10 years old. Use simple words and an analogy."

Use this for: understanding new concepts, teaching others, learning quickly.

5. Improve Your Writing

"Improve the following text. Make it clearer, more engaging, and fix any grammar issues: [paste your text]"

Use this for: essays, reports, social media posts, cover letters, presentations.
