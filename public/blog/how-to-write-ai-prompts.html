How to Write AI Prompts That Get Perfect Results Every Time

Published 2026-06-03 · Anglotec AI Masterclass

Writing great AI prompts is a skill anyone can learn. This guide breaks down the science of prompt engineering into simple, actionable steps.

The 5-Step Prompt Formula

Step 1: Define the Role

Start by telling the AI who it is. "You are an expert [role] with 20 years of experience in [field]." This immediately frames the response quality.

Step 2: Provide Context

Give the AI background information. The more relevant context, the better the output. Think of it as briefing a consultant.

Step 3: Be Specific About the Task

Vague requests get vague answers. Instead of "write about dogs," try "write a 500-word guide about training golden retriever puppies for first-time owners."

Step 4: Specify the Format

Tell the AI exactly how you want the output: bullet points, table, essay, code, JSON, etc.

Step 5: Set Constraints

Define what's in and out: word count, tone (professional/casual), audience level, things to avoid.

Before and After Examples

Bad: "Write about marketing."

Good: "You are a digital marketing consultant. Write a 300-word email to small business owners explaining 3 low-cost social media strategies for restaurants. Include a call-to-action. Tone: friendly and encouraging."
