5 Steps to Master ChatGPT Prompting

1

Start with Basic Prompts

Begin with everyday tasks: writing emails, summarizing articles, brainstorming ideas. Simple prompts like "Write a professional email requesting a meeting" build foundational skills.

2

Learn the Structure

Great prompts have structure: context + task + format + constraints. Example: "You are a marketing expert (context). Write 5 social media headlines (task) under 50 characters each (format) for a fitness app targeting millennials (constraints)."

3

Practice with 3,000 Flashcards

5

The best learning happens through application. Use your new prompting skills on actual projects — writing code, creating content, analyzing data, planning strategy. Our prompts are designed for real-world use.
