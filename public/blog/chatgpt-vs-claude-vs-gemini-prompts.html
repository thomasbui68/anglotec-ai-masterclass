ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini: Which AI Handles Prompts Best?

Published 2026-06-05 · Anglotec AI Masterclass

Not all AI models handle prompts the same way. This comparison breaks down how ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini respond to different prompt types — so you know which tool to use.

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Best for: General tasks, coding, creative writing, multi-step reasoning.

Prompt tip: Be explicit about format. ChatGPT follows structured instructions well.

Claude (Anthropic)

Best for: Long documents, analysis, safety-critical tasks, nuanced reasoning.

Prompt tip: Claude excels with long context. Provide detailed background for best results.

Gemini (Google)

Best for: Multimodal tasks (text + images), real-time info, Google integration.

Prompt tip: Use for tasks requiring current information or image analysis.
